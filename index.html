About

Heart.
Grit.
Rayne.

Somewhere between Nashville and nowhere, Dakota Rayne makes songs where truth and fiction blur.

Her music carries the warmth of Southern nights, the edge of a storm rolling in and stories that feel familiar even when you have never lived them.

"I'm as real as you need me to be. Lose yourself in the music and forget what you don't know."

Equal parts heart, grit and trouble.

Dakota Rayne is an AI-created country music project built around original music, visual storytelling and an ever-growing fictional world.
